How this estimate works +

Jev reads the displayed source evidence and FRED observations. It supplies a distribution over a rate cut, hold, or hike at the next scheduled meeting. These are experimental subjective estimates, not validated probabilities. The source selection is incomplete and no futures-market baseline is included.

Dollar impacts use fixed, illustrative one-week ETF returns for each outcome. We weight those scenario calculations by Jev's probabilities. This is not a statistically estimated return distribution. The same rate decision can produce a different reaction depending on expectations and guidance.

Source availability & freshness +

Fetched on demand and cached for five minutes. Observation dates and market timestamps can be older than retrieval time. When FRED blocks the hosting network, a verified copy retained for at most 72 hours is explicitly labeled with its retrieval date. Other source failures stay unavailable. No invented values are substituted. Yahoo prices may be delayed and are a prototype integration.

See assumptions & limitations +

These are hand-authored stress scenarios, not forecasts or live market data. No probability is assigned. Returns are fixed assumptions, not statistical confidence intervals. The calculation is the sum of each holding's weight multiplied by its assumed return.

The comparison transfers allocation from SPY to cash. Cash assumes zero return over the week. Taxes, fees, slippage, dividends, and intraperiod price movements are excluded. Lower losses in one scenario can mean lower gains in another.
