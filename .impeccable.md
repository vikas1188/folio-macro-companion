## Design Context
### Users
US retail investors holding US stocks and ETFs; understand upcoming macro exposure and compare allocation changes.
### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, companionable. Working name: Folio.
### Aesthetic Direction
Apple-inspired object-led scrolling: warm white, graphite, optical blue, oversized restrained typography. One persistent sculptural portfolio sphere moves and zooms through the narrative, then settles beside a usable simulator.
### Design Principles
- Motion explains the relationship between evidence, events, and the portfolio.
- Immediate visible feedback for every action.
- Scenario assumptions and illustrative status are explicit.
- Full keyboard, mobile, and reduced-motion support.
- No invented live feeds or financial certainty.
